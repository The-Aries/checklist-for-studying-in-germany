# Content Research and Maintenance Plan

## Current milestone note

The broader product covers APS through the first residence permit. APS + visa were frozen in Milestone 01; City Registration + Residence Permit were subsequently researched and implemented on 2026-09-22. The pre-departure section remains future guidance and must not be implemented as an empty or guessed page.

## 1. Objective

Before implementation is considered complete, every actionable requirement in the first-version checklist must be derived from current authoritative sources and recorded in a traceable way.

## 2. Broader product research sequence

Research these sections in order:

1. APS
2. German student visa for the target audience
3. Pre-departure transition items that are directly useful for later official procedures
4. Anmeldung
5. Student residence permit
6. FAQ derived from common, sourceable questions

## 3. APS

Use the current APS China material for the general domestic applicant procedure and narrow the public checklist to the defined audience: Mainland China bachelor's graduates applying for a German master's degree.

Capture:

- registration/application steps;
- fees/payment requirements;
- required graduate documents;
- language evidence;
- mailing/submission requirements;
- interview/TestAS distinctions only insofar as they apply to or affect the target audience;
- DigZert/result handling;
- explicit conditional items within the target audience, including time-since-graduation requirements;
- official templates when supplied by APS.

If an official template exists, link to it rather than publishing a copied unofficial substitute.

If no official template exists and a convenience template is later created by this project, label it clearly as non-official.

## 4. Visa

Primary sources should be the German Missions in China and other competent German federal sources.

Capture only requirements relevant to the defined target audience and make clear when a requirement may depend on personal circumstances.

Avoid relying on commercial visa agencies or social-media summaries as normative sources.

## 5. Pre-departure

Keep this section short and procedural. It is not a packing guide.

Candidate categories:

- verify issued visa details;
- keep required originals available for travel/arrival;
- retain secure digital copies;
- ensure health-insurance timing is understood;
- confirm accommodation documentation;
- understand how to obtain/use `Wohnungsgeberbestätigung`;
- identify local Anmeldung office and appointment process;
- identify local Ausländerbehörde/residence-permit process.

Only include an item when it has a clear practical relationship to the official post-arrival process.

## 6. Anmeldung

Use German official sources for the nationally common legal/procedural core.

Do not make one city's appointment method, form, or office workflow appear universal.

Where useful, a city-specific source may be stored as a labeled implementation example during research, but the public v1 checklist should stay general.

## 7. Residence permit

Use federal/common legal guidance plus municipal authority pages to identify a stable common denominator.

Public content must distinguish:

- generally common requirements;
- local forms/appointments/extra evidence that must be checked with the applicant's own Ausländerbehörde.

Do not turn one city's checklist into a national rule.

## 8. Terminology

Define terms in one concise sentence inside the relevant checklist row or nearby note.

Preferred source order:

1. official authority definition;
2. official German government information portal;
3. reputable general reference only if needed.

Avoid separate glossary pages in v1.

## 9. Local source archive

Research copies of public source documents may be downloaded to:

`local/references/`

This directory is intentionally ignored by Git.

The public repository stores source metadata and original URLs in `docs/SOURCES.md`, not redistributed copies of third-party source documents unless there is a specific licensing/reliability reason to do so.

For each source record:

- source ID;
- title;
- responsible authority;
- canonical URL;
- retrieved date;
- source publication/version date if available;
- language;
- relevant pages/sections;
- which checklist section/items it supports;
- notes about expiry/replacement if known.

## 10. Maintenance rule

`Last Updated` changes whenever public checklist content is materially edited.

`Last Verified` changes only after the relevant authoritative sources have actually been re-checked.

Verification must not be advanced merely because formatting/code changed.
