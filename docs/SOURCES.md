## Post-arrival common-core verification — 2026-09-22

The Ulm university handout supplied by the maintainer was used only as a comparison aid. Its overall flow matches the Germany-wide pattern, but some details are local rather than universal.

For City Registration, §§17 and 19 BMG establish the nationwide two-week registration deadline and `Wohnungsgeberbestätigung`; the Bundesportal confirms that the competent office and implementation details depend on the selected region. The Ulm handout's enrolment-certificate requirement was therefore **not** promoted to a nationwide checklist requirement.

For the study Residence Permit, §16b AufenthG establishes the legal basis. A current Bundesportal service page updated 2026-09-01 lists the common core documents (passport, biometric photo, livelihood, enrolment, health insurance), while the federal `Make it in Germany` January-2026 procedure explicitly places address registration before the local foreigners-authority application and says to apply before the entry visa expires. Cross-checks against current Berlin and Cologne authority pages showed the same core but additional local document/process requirements, so the public site keeps the nationwide checklist compact and directs users to their own `Ausländerbehörde` for local details.
