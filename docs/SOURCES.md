# Source Register

This file is the public source ledger for the project.

Research PDFs/web captures may be retained locally under `local/references/`, which is ignored by Git. Public checklist content should link to the current canonical official source wherever possible.

## Implementation-start freshness check — 2026-09-19

All eleven canonical URLs were re-opened. Fresh downloads of APS-001 through APS-006 and VISA-001 through VISA-004 are byte-identical (SHA-256) to the content-freeze archives. VISA-003 still links the January 2025 study checklist. No material requirement change was found.

The direct VISA-005 download returns a browser-verification challenge, including in the earlier local archive; that HTML is not evidence of policy content. The official page was separately read through web retrieval: “Requirements for the visa for studying in Germany”, living-cost bullet, still specifies EUR 11,904 for 2026. VISA-002 p. 2 independently confirms EUR 992/month. Local fresh copies and comparison evidence remain private under `local/references/freshness/`.

Official form links exposed by VISA-001, “所需材料”, were also retained in the dataset: [VIDEX](https://videx.diplo.de/videx/visum-erfassung/videx-langfristiger-aufenthalt) and [Section 54 declaration](https://china.diplo.de/resource/blob/2561252/5e9cccec777a2ca0b7aaa019dcd6f670/belehrung-zum-visumantrag-data.pdf). These are official links, distinct from the project's explicitly unofficial CV template.
